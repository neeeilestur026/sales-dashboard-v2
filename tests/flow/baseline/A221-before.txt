A SEPARATE, LARGER PROBLEM THIS BASELINE EXPOSES — NOT IN A221's SCOPE
  The trial balance does not balance in the ordinary sense and cannot, because the two sides of the
  procure-to-pay chain post in DIFFERENT UNITS:

    JE-PO-2026-39 Chicago Pneumatics Tools   Dr 1400 Purchases Clearing  202     <- USD, currency 'USD'
    JE-APPAY-AP-202607-006                   Cr 1010 Cash            310,895.71  <- PHP

  A foreign PO whose 'Exchange Rate' is 0 and whose totalPHP is blank posts its journal at the
  FOREIGN CURRENCY total, while its payment posts in pesos. Hence Accounts Payable — a liability —
  carries a DEBIT balance of 1,093,075.44, and Purchases Clearing (which should net to roughly zero)
  carries a credit balance of 458,706.70.

  Correcting AP-202607-006 removes 298,448.47 of the Cash distortion but CANNOT fix this: the
  remaining imbalance is structural and belongs to the PO journal, not to the payment. Flagged here
  so that "the trial balance still looks wrong afterwards" is understood as expected, not as A221
  having failed.
