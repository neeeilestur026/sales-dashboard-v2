Money that reaches nobody

Commission reaches a rep down one chain — collection → sales order → quotation → owner — and it can break at any link. Leave the box empty to scan every collection, or name a quotation or sales order to ask why that one cannot be claimed.

Collected payments you can claim

A claim is made against the actual payments received, less withholding tax. Tick the payments you are claiming for and file the request — the director and management see the same figures you do, including how much of the order has been collected so far.
