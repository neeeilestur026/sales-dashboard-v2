Fills your daily report's email log with the emails you sent today.

Your full GoDaddy webmail address, for example sales@hi-escorp.com.
The password is encrypted before storage and is never shown again to your browser, the spreadsheet, or anyone else after this page.
How it works. Your password is sent over HTTPS to our server, used to verify the connection, then encrypted with AES-128 before storage. The encryption key is held only by the server. Nothing in your browser, your spreadsheet, or any log can read your stored password.
