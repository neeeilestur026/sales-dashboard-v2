Commissions for Payroll

Approved commissions grouped by salary cutoff — the figure to enter as an Incentive for each person, with Commission as the category. Entered that way it is paid once, in that cutoff only, and appears in the employee's incentive history.

Match these names by eye. The commission records and the payroll register are separate systems that do not share employee identities — one holds a full name, the other “Surname, First name”. Nothing here matches them automatically, on purpose: a wrong automatic match would pay the wrong person without anyone noticing.
